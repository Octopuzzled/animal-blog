FAQ

What is this blog about?

This blog is about animals in all their variety: pets, wildlife and everything in between. You will find personal stories, surprising facts and practical tips from people who love animals.

Where do the facts and stories come from?

Stories come from our own experiences with animals. Facts are checked against reliable sources such as scientific studies and conservation organisations before we publish them.

How can brands, shelters, or fellow creators collaborate with me or get in touch?

We are open to collaborations with animal shelters, conservation projects, brands and other creators. Send a message through the contact form below, or suggest an animal or topic you would like to read about.

How can readers support this blog and the work behind it?

Share posts with friends, follow us on social media and tell us what you would like to see next. If you can, also support your local animal shelter or a conservation project.
